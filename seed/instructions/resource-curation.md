# External resource curation

- Curate a specific, trustworthy HTTP/HTTPS page, not a generic portal.
- Explain what it covers, why it is useful, and when to use it.
- Tag only its primary learning value.
- External resources never contain lesson bodies, objectives, or review
  candidates and never acquire LearningContent progress semantics.
- Draft first. Move to `learning-content/resources/` only after human review.
